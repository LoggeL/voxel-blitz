Primary request: olive-drab painted industrial metal surface, medium desaturated yellow-green olive paint (#778059), with subtle powder-coated grain, shallow worn scuffs, tiny scattered chips exposing muted steel, faint variations in paint thickness. The material must remain legible when mapped onto faceted game geometry; no dramatic damage or large marks.
