Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production 3D weapon design reference sheet for the video game VOXEL BLITZ
Primary request: Redesign the fictional RX-8 HAVOC single-shot shoulder-fired recoilless RPG launcher for first-person play. Design direction RIDGEBACK: a compact, sculpted industrial launcher built around a fat mid-body and a long slim launch tube. The receiver is a chamfered olive-drab armored monocoque with a raised dorsal spine, slotted side service panels and an integrated carry handle that doubles as a low optical sight bridge. The forward half is a gunmetal perforated thermal shroud with hexagonal cutouts revealing a dark inner tube and an orange heat band; the support hand grips an angled polymer foregrip below it. The muzzle ends in a thick castellated blast collar with a seated ivory-and-orange rocket warhead visible in the mouth. The rear ends in a compact four-lobed De Laval blast brake, not a huge open bucket, with a vertical-hinge loading gate on the left flank. Raked pistol grip with rubber panels, orange-tipped mechanical arming lever on the right, underslung cylindrical ignition canister, contoured rubber shoulder pad. Palette: olive drab, dark phosphated gunmetal, safety orange, warm ivory, molded black polymer, pebbled rubber.
Composition/framing: dark slate studio concept sheet. Large clean 3/4 front-left hero view in the center. Side orthographic view below it. Top-left inset: first-person view showing a slim, unobtrusive silhouette with an open sight channel and the rear venturi kept compact and below the sight line. Top-right inset: open-breech reload with the left-side gate swung clear and a complete finned rocket round sliding into the bore. Small palette swatches bottom-left. Modelled ivory RX-8 HAVOC stencil on the receiver flank.
Constraints: fictional game prop only; no oversized rear stovepipe blocking the view; no flat white top plank; no external diagrams, arrows or dimension numbers; readable hard-surface forms that can be modeled in Blender; consistent proportions for a 1.35 m launcher with a 0.175 m sight line above the bore.
