Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production 3D weapon design reference sheet for the video game VOXEL BLITZ
Primary request: Redesign the fictional RX-8 HAVOC single-shot shoulder-fired recoilless RPG launcher for first-person play. Design direction BRUTUS: a chunky armored break-action launcher with an unmistakable silhouette. A short rectangular olive-drab receiver with heavy chamfered ribs and two large dark polymer side pockets sits over a slim gunmetal launch tube. The forward section is a skeletal blast cage: four thick longitudinal rails around the exposed tube with orange warning bands and a massive castellated muzzle ring; an ivory/orange rocket nose is seated in the mouth. The top has a prominent armored optic box with an orange lens, protected by twin carry-handle ears, plus a folding iron sight. The rear is a squared four-port blast brake inside a protective cage, with a vertical-hinge loading gate on the left flank that swings the whole rear assembly clear. A big raked pistol grip, a vertical foregrip with rubber ribs, a folding shoulder rest and an underslung cylindrical ignition canister sell the weight. Right-flank orange-tipped arming lever. Palette: olive drab, dark phosphated gunmetal, safety orange, warm ivory, molded black polymer, pebbled rubber.
Composition/framing: dark slate studio concept sheet. Large 3/4 front-right hero view. Lower side orthographic view. Top-left first-person inset showing the optic box and muzzle aligned with the reticle, rear cage low and unobtrusive. Top-right open-breech inset with the left-side gate open and a complete finned rocket round entering the bore. Small palette swatches. Modelled RX-8 HAVOC stencil.
Constraints: fictional game prop only; no enormous open rear drum; no flat white top deck; no diagram arrows or dimension numbers; readable forms buildable in Blender; proportions for a 1.35 m launcher with a 0.175 m sight line.
