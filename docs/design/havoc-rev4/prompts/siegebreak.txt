Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production 3D weapon design reference sheet for the video game VOXEL BLITZ
Primary request: Redesign the fictional RX-8 HAVOC single-shot shoulder-fired recoilless RPG launcher for first-person play. Design direction SIEGEBREAK: a refined classic anti-armor silhouette with modern industrial detailing. A long gunmetal launch tube runs the full length, wrapped by two olive-drab armor saddles with chamfered edges and ivory stencil plates; the forward heat section is bare phosphated steel with a perforated protective half-shroud over the top only, leaving the support grip clear. A large hooded muzzle collar exposes the ivory/orange nose of a loaded rocket. The top carries a cantilevered digital sight housing with an orange reflex lens, a low U-notch backup and a stout carry handle. The rear is a compact conical venturi with a ring of cooling slots and a hinged left-flank loading gate, sized no wider than the receiver. A raked pistol grip with rubber panels, an angled foregrip, a folding shoulder rest, a right-flank orange-tipped arming lever and an underslung ignition canister complete the furniture. Palette: olive drab, dark phosphated gunmetal, safety orange, warm ivory, molded black polymer, pebbled rubber.
Composition/framing: dark charcoal studio concept sheet. Large 3/4 side hero view. Lower side orthographic profile. Top-right first-person inset showing the tube leading cleanly to the center reticle, the digital sight housing low in frame and the rear venturi out of the way. Top-left open-breech inset showing the left-side gate open and a complete finned rocket cartridge being loaded. Small palette swatches. Modelled RX-8 HAVOC markings.
Constraints: fictional game prop only; no giant open rear bucket; no flat white slab; no diagram arrows or dimensions; production-ready hard-surface forms modelable in Blender; proportions for a 1.35 m launcher with a 0.175 m sight line.
