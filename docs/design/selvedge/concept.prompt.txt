Design reference for Voxel Blitz. Photoreal studio product concept for a fictional portable field loom called “SL-2 SELVEDGE”. Landscape 3:2 composition on a warm gray workbench; one large, crisp orthographic-like three-quarter view from the bobbin side, with an inset macro of the filament escapement. Instrument-grade dark petrol anodized frame, exposed broadside spool of burnt-orange coppery filament, chamfered ivory ceramic tuning fork at the front with a clearly open channel between two parallel tines, visible capstan, sprung clicker, maker's data plate and fine engraved registration marks, worn satin bearing surfaces, tactile rear grip and forward handhold. One bright short strand makes the route from spool to ceramic guide legible. Restrained industrial design, soft grazing light and realistic roughness, legible silhouette. An optical surveying instrument from a fictional voxel textile workshop. No human, no shells, no conventional barrel, no editorial labels, no branding other than the small model designation.
