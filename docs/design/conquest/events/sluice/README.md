# Ford sluices HUD

Feature: the two ford sluices on Frontier (the old weir across the north reach above ford-north, a twin above ford-south) each hold a pond one voxel over the river; blowing a sluice's piers and gate leaves releases the pond as a bore and scours its ford two voxels deeper, closing it to every hull for the round (docs/conquest.md "Ford sluices"). The HUD has to say which sluice is failing, that the ford below it is gone for vehicles, and where the water runs.

- `sluice-a/b/c-reference.png` + `.prompt.txt`: gpt-image-2 alternatives.
  - **A (chosen)**: the framework's objective banner in red with a wave-glyph badge, "FORD-NORTH SLUICE · WASHED OUT / NO HULL FORDS · VEHICLES CROSS AT THE BRIDGES", the event hexagon over the weir reading "N SLUICE · WASHED OUT", and on the minimap the ford painted as deep water with white break marks at both bank ends and a white foam line across the reach where the bore is.
  - B: a tactical-map treatment with crossing tags ("FORD-NORTH · NO HULLS" struck through, "S SLUICE · 40 %" in amber). Dropped: a second label per crossing on top of the event marker, and the big map already shows flags and roads densely; the struck tag is the same information as the break marks.
  - C (kept for the `warning` stage): the amber "FAILING / GET OUT OF THE FORD · BREAKS IN 2 S" banner with its countdown bar and the weir in hazard stripes on the minimap. This is what the levolution presenter already draws; the hatched bore path on the minimap was left out (the push zones are what the bots avoid, the players read the banner).
- `captures/`: the implementation (`node tools/conquest-hud-capture.mjs --only sluice-washed,sluice-map --sizes desktop,portrait --out docs/design/conquest/events/sluice/captures`, muted CDP), desktop and portrait, 12 s into the release (the bore 60 m down the reach).

Compared with A: same banner placement, tone, glyph badge and texts; the marker carries the authoritative distance ("45 M"). The washed-out ford, the break marks and the foam line are drawn from the decoded `ev` row and `sluiceBore` (the same front the server's push zones use), so they show on the minimap and the full map; at the minimap's 170 m range the ford is about 20 px wide and the marks stay small. The capture page renders a fixed world backdrop, so the 3D scene in the captures does not show the weir or the rubble.
