Game art design reference sheet, 3D voxel (Minecraft-like, 0.2 m cubes) military vehicles from a Battlefield-style browser shooter, rendered in a clean stylised lit voxel look on a grassy green voxel field under a pale blue sky. Two teams face each other: WEST team vehicles wear olive woodland camouflage (olive, mid green, black-green blots), EAST team vehicles wear sand desert camouflage (sand, tan, dark khaki blots). Show, for each team, a main battle tank, an attack helicopter (narrow gunship with stub wings and rocket pods), and an open jeep, side by side so the two teams can be compared, plus a small inset of the same pair seen from 150 m away and one seen from above. The current problem: the only team marks are tiny blue (WEST) or orange (EAST) roundels and a thin white/black stripe, so at range the vehicles are hard to tell apart. Design goal: make the team of every vehicle instantly readable at 150 m and from the air, while keeping a grounded military look. No text, no logos, no letters.

Variant C, two-tone sections: the nose / front glacis and the tail section of each vehicle are painted solid in the team colour — WEST cobalt blue (#2d5ad8), EAST signal orange (#ec6a22) — while the middle keeps its camouflage; the helicopter tail boom, fin and rotor tips are team colour, the tank turret roof and gun mantlet are team colour, the jeep bonnet is team colour.
