Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: vehicle damage-state sheet for a browser voxel shooter. Subjects: a main battle tank (angular turret, long gun with slotted muzzle brake, segmented side skirts) in WEST woodland camouflage and a utility transport helicopter (wide open side doors, door guns) in EAST desert camouflage. All vehicles are built from 0.2 m voxels with greedy-meshed flat faces, crisp ambient occlusion and a thin light edge highlight on every convex edge. WEST team wears woodland camouflage (olive and dark green blots, white team stripe, blue roundel); EAST team wears desert camouflage (sand, tan and dark khaki blots, black team stripe, orange roundel). Three-quarter hero view, warm low sun, neutral grey ground.

The same tank and transport, each in four states left to right: intact; damaged (soot creeping across the paint, a smoke trail from the engine deck); burning (flames and embers from the engine, dark smoke); and wreck (fully charred, lamps dark, turret or rotor torn off, a tall smoke column).
No branding, no text except small state labels.
