# Tracked tank model

`makeTankModel(materials)` in `public/js/vehicles/models/tank.js` builds a procedural, faceted tank in metres. Forward is -Z and track cleats rest at approximately y=0.013. The returned hull must retain unit scale. Paint, steel, rubber, glazing and light materials are supplied by the vehicle renderer; armor UVs support its repeating paint texture and team colors.

The chassis has sloped upper and lower glacis armor, separate fenders and segmented side skirts, six roadwheels per side, front idlers and rear drive sprockets. Extruded belt rings retain their open silhouette. Forty-two steel cleats per side use one instanced mesh per belt. Rear engine louvers, stowage, exhaust, tow fittings, driver's hatch and a shovel distinguish the front and rear.

The turret is a child of the hull at y=1.66, z=-0.19. Its sloped cheeks and rear bustle rotate together around local Y. The gun is a child of the turret at y=0.4, z=-1.0 and pitches around local X, with positive rotation elevating the barrel. Mantlet, root, sleeve, barrel and muzzle brake move together. Commander cupola, vision slot, machine gun, smoke launchers and antenna stay with turret yaw. Each of the 22 wheel groups rotates around X using its own spin radius: twelve roadwheels, four end wheels and six return rollers. The roadwheels and end wheels have separated circles inside the belts. Authored armor faces have outward winding.

Full visual bounds at neutral aim are approximately 3.79 m wide, 7.01 m long including the barrel, and 2.86 m tall including the antenna. The armored hull is 5.10 m long; turret roof is 2.37 m, commander hatch is 2.57 m, and the machine gun reaches 2.77 m. The narrow aerial reaches 2.88 m. The barrel projects about 1.9 m beyond the front glacis, with its pivot-to-muzzle distance 3.2405 m.

Recommended gameplay collider settings are radius 2.65 m and height 2.65 m, treating the barrel, machine gun and aerial as visual attachments. A radius of 2.65 does not enclose every corner of the rectangular track footprint: a fully conservative circle would need approximately 3.17 m. Use the requested 2.65 m gameplay clearance consistently in server and client, or use an oriented body box for exact hull collision.

`node tools/conquest-tank-model-test.mjs` verifies the 118 meshes, provided material ownership, finite vertices, unit hull scale, ground origin, vehicle dimensions, belt pair, 12 roadwheels, 22 spin pivots, wheel clearance, outward armor faces, independent yaw and gun pitch, and the enclosed driver/gunner anchors. The factory returns `animateTracks(leftDistance, rightDistance)`, accepting signed per-frame travel in metres and moving each belt’s instanced cleats around the full perimeter while its shell stays rigid. It also returns a `muzzle` attachment and `muzzleLength: 3.2405`, `gunPivotHeight: 2.06`, `gunPivotForward: 1.19` for matching projectile origins. The animation test verifies independent left and right travel. Actual desktop and mobile browser qualification belongs to the integrating vehicle renderer.
