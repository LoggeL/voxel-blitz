Concept art reference for a second Conquest battlefield in a browser voxel shooter. Same art style as the existing map "Frontier": chunky 1 m Minecraft-like voxels with pixel-art block textures, warm baked lighting, soft distance fog, layered blocky mountain ranges on the horizon, readable game screenshot framing from about 12 m above ground, 16:10, no HUD panels.

Map identity: "REDROCK CANYON", an arid red-rock desert. A river flows north to south through the middle of the map: in the north it is held back by a big concrete gravity dam with a control tower and a stepped spillway (flag C, "Redrock Dam"), forming a turquoise reservoir lake; south of the dam the river runs in a deep canyon with banded red, orange and cream sandstone walls (stepped strata, about 14 m deep), crossed by a long steel truss trestle bridge. Flat-topped red mesas and tall sandstone buttes stand on the dusty plain; saguaro cacti, sage scrub, a palm oasis with adobe houses on one side, a copper mine headframe tower and grey tailings heaps on the other, a gravel road and a paved road leading to the dam crest. Late afternoon desert sun, long shadows, warm haze. Battlefield-style capture-point markers (small circles with the letters C and D, white and orange) floating over the far landmarks, like the current game.
