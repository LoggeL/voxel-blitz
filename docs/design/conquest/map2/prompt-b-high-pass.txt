Concept art reference for a second Conquest battlefield in a browser voxel shooter. Same art style as the existing map "Frontier": chunky 1 m Minecraft-like voxels with pixel-art block textures, baked lighting, soft distance fog, layered blocky mountain ranges, game screenshot framing from about 12 m above ground, 16:10, no HUD panels.

Map identity: "HIGH PASS", a snowy alpine mountain pass. A frozen river with ice floes runs through a high valley between snow-covered voxel slopes and dark pine forests; a stone avalanche gallery, a timber ski lodge village, a cable-car pylon line, a military radar station on a ridge and a concrete hydro power station. A switchback road climbs to a narrow saddle in the middle where flag C sits beside an old fortress. Cold blue morning light, crisp shadows, drifting snow particles. Battlefield-style capture-point markers (small circles with the letters C and D, white and orange) floating over the far landmarks, like the current game.
