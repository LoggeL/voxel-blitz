Concept art reference for a second Conquest battlefield in a browser voxel shooter. Same art style as the existing map "Frontier": chunky 1 m Minecraft-like voxels with pixel-art block textures, warm baked lighting, soft distance fog, blocky ranges on the horizon, game screenshot framing from about 12 m above ground, 16:10, no HUD panels.

Map identity: "SALT MESA", a pale desert basin: a huge bright white salt flat with a cracked crust in the middle, a dry riverbed (wadi) with a thin creek winding across it, tall tan mesas with flat tops, a railway line with a water tower and freight cars, a radar dome on a mesa, a crashed airliner fuselage, adobe ruins and oil derricks. Noon sun, pale blue sky with heat haze, very open long sightlines. Battlefield-style capture-point markers (small circles with the letters C and D, white and orange) floating over the far landmarks, like the current game.
