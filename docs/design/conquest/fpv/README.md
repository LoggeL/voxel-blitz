# Engineer FPV drone feed

Request: an Engineer FPV kamikaze drone with a first-person feed HUD showing the battery, signal bars, speed, altitude and the distance to the operator, with static that grows as the signal fades.

- `ref-a/b/c-*.jpg` + `prompt-*.txt`: gpt-image-2 alternatives. The prompts describe the current Conquest HUD (the top ticket bar, the navy panel style and the team colours, as in `docs/design/conquest/redesign/captures/hud/`).
  - A: corner telemetry panels (battery and signal top left, SPD / ALT / DIST top right) and a pitch tape at the bottom. The top corners are where the squad list, the banners and the weather tab sit on phones.
  - B: an OSD drawn straight onto the video with speed and altitude tapes, as real goggles do. It reads well, but white text on bright sky has no backing, and the tapes cover the edges where the flag markers clamp.
  - **C (chosen)**: one instrument strip at the bottom centre in the vehicle-card style: FPV DRONE with the drones left, BATTERY (seconds and an amber bar), SIGNAL (four bars), SPEED, ALT and RANGE. A small square reticle sits in the centre, with a soft vignette and light analog static. It reuses the vehicle-card look the player already knows, keeps the corners free and fits a phone as a single row.
- `captures/`: the implementation (`node tools/conquest-hud-capture.mjs --only fpv --sizes desktop,portrait --out docs/design/conquest/fpv/captures`), desktop 1440×900 and phone portrait 390×844, with no HUD overlaps.

Compared with C: same strip, same order and colour split (amber battery bar, own-blue signal bars, amber and then red as the battery or the signal runs low). Added from A and B: a goggle vignette with scanlines, analog static (fresh noise every frame, more of it as the signal drops past 250 m) and a thin horizon bar that tilts against the drone's bank. While the drone flies, the infantry cards (HP, ammo, grenades, the crosshair), the minimap, the squad list and the capture ring are hidden. The ticket bar, the flag and unit markers and the kill feed stay. The key hints show for 6 s after a launch rather than permanently (the HUD rule: no permanent keybind line). On phones the strip sits under the ticket bar, since the sticks and buttons own the bottom edge. The touch MORTAR button becomes FPV on foot and CUT while flying. The static capture page has no live 3D drone camera, so its backdrop is the usual fixture scene seen from the drone's position.
