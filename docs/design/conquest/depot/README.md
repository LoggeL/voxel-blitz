# Forward service depot HUD

Feature: four forward service depots, two per battlefield (docs/conquest.md "Forward service depots"). Each repairs and rearms the hulls of the side holding its flag until it is blown up. The HUD has to show three things: which depots serve which side, that the player's hull is being serviced right now, and that a depot is gone. The chain reaction keeps its own design (`docs/design/conquest/events/depot/`).

- `depot-a/b/c-reference.png` + `.prompt.txt`: gpt-image-2 alternatives (medium quality, 1536x1024).
  - **A (chosen), "service strip"**: a hexagon badge with a wrench crossed over a jerry can on the maps, in the colour of the side that holds the depot's flag, and grey and faded for a destroyed depot. The vehicle card gets a green SERVICING pill with the same glyph, the HP bar glows green with a light sweep, and one grey line HULL +5 %/S sits under it. A small DEPOT world marker stands over the canopy.
  - B, "service ring": a round badge with a dashed 14 m service circle on the minimap, a full-width green header strip "SERVICING · DEPOT E" on the vehicle card, moving chevrons in the HP bar and "+1" ticks on the weapon rows. Dropped: the header strip pushes the card taller (on a phone it then reaches the minimap), the dashed circle is under 2 px at the minimap's scale, and the wire has no per-round rearm events for the ticks.
  - C, "pit-stop ring": a large green progress ring under the crosshair, "SERVICING 64 %" and "HOLD POSITION", a square minimap badge. Dropped: the ring covers the aim point while the tank may still be fighting from the forecourt, and the square badge breaks the event hexagon language of the maps.
- `captures/`: the implementation (`node tools/conquest-hud-capture.mjs --only depot-service --out docs/design/conquest/depot/captures`, muted CDP) at desktop 1440x900, phone portrait 390x844 and phone landscape 844x390. `desktop-depot-service-zoom.png` is a 2x crop of the desktop vehicle card and a 3x crop of the minimap. The capture page draws the HUD over a static map backdrop, so the 3D scene in the shots is not the depot.

Compared with A:

- Same: the SERVICING pill (green, the `depot-service` glyph), the green glow on the HP bar with the sweep (CSS, `prefers-reduced-motion` keeps the glow only), and the service line, which reads DEPOT · HULL +5 %/S, or DEPOT · REARMING once the HP is full and a magazine is still filling. The hexagon badge in the owner's tone sits at the pump on the minimap and the full map. It shows the pool's lost share as its arc and pulses slowly while a hull is serviced. The enemy depot is orange, a neutral flag's depot grey and faded.
- Different: there is no DEPOT world marker over a standing depot. The framework shows world markers only for live phases, and four permanent markers would crowd the flag markers. The badge on the maps and the card cover it. The reference's "REARM 4 S" countdown is not shown: the wire has no rearm timer for the depot, and the card reads only the hull row's `servicing` status bit and HP.
- On the minimap, the own depot's badge sits under the player arrow while the hull is on the forecourt, which is 9 m from the pump. The enemy badge at the edge keeps its glyph legible.
