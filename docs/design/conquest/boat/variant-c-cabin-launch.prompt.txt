Game art design reference sheet, 3D voxel (Minecraft-like, 0.2 m cubes, flat-shaded with soft ambient occlusion) military patrol launch from a Battlefield-style browser shooter. Same look as the game's existing voxel jeep and tank: chunky readable shapes, olive woodland camouflage (olive #687341 ground with mid green #465a2e, olive-brown #5c4b30 and black-green #29361f blots), dark charcoal fittings, gunmetal guns, and saturated team recognition panels. Show the WEST team boat (woodland camo, cobalt blue #2a5ce6 recognition panels) as a 3/4 front hero view floating on a flat voxel river (teal-green water cubes, 24 m wide, grassy voxel banks with reeds), plus a small side view, a top-down view, and the EAST team version (sand desert camo #c9a066 with tan #a57a49 and dark khaki #6b5235 blots, signal orange #f0661c recognition panels) in an inset seen from 150 m. No text, no logos, no letters.

Variant C, armoured patrol launch: a 9 m long, 2.6 m wide angular steel hull with slab sides angled inward and a chined bow, a low open-backed armoured wheelhouse forward of amidships with narrow vision slits and an open rear where the driver stands, a .50 heavy machine gun on a ring mount in front of the wheelhouse on the foredeck behind a box-shaped shield, the gunner standing exposed. Aft an open well deck with low armoured bulwarks where two exposed passengers sit on side benches, a stern engine hatch with two dark exhaust stacks, a short mast with a whip antenna. Recognition: the whole wheelhouse roof painted as a big cobalt panel (visible from the air), a cobalt band on the wheelhouse sides and along the bulwarks, and a cobalt transom. A roundel on each bow flank. White bow wave and a foamy wake.
