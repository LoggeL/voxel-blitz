Game art design reference sheet, 3D voxel (Minecraft-like, 0.2 m cubes, flat-shaded with soft ambient occlusion) military patrol boat from a Battlefield-style browser shooter. Same look as the game's existing voxel jeep and tank: chunky readable shapes, olive woodland camouflage (olive #687341 ground with mid green #465a2e, olive-brown #5c4b30 and black-green #29361f blots), dark charcoal fittings, gunmetal guns, and saturated team recognition panels. Show the WEST team boat (woodland camo, cobalt blue #2a5ce6 recognition panels) as a 3/4 front hero view floating on a flat voxel river (teal-green water cubes, 24 m wide, grassy voxel banks with reeds), plus a small side view, a top-down view, and the EAST team version (sand desert camo #c9a066 with tan #a57a49 and dark khaki #6b5235 blots, signal orange #f0661c recognition panels) in an inset seen from 150 m. No text, no logos, no letters.

Variant A, riverine patrol boat (PBR style): a 9 m long, 2.6 m wide open fibreglass hull with a sharp raked bow and a flat planing stern, low gunwales about 0.9 m above the waterline. At the bow a gun tub ring with a .50 heavy machine gun on a pintle behind a small curved gun shield, the gunner standing exposed. Amidships a small open helm console with a short tinted windscreen and a steering wheel for the driver, standing exposed. Aft an open cockpit with two bench seats for exposed passengers facing outward, grab rails along both sides, and a twin waterjet / outboard housing at the transom with a dark exhaust. A low radar mast with a whip antenna behind the console. Recognition: a big square cobalt panel on the console roof (visible from the air), a wide cobalt band along both flanks of the hull just below the gunwale, and the transom in the team colour. A small roundel (blue on white ring) on each bow flank. Black rubber fender strips along the waterline. White bow wave and a foamy wake behind the stern.
