Game art design reference sheet, 3D voxel (Minecraft-like, 0.2 m cubes, flat-shaded with soft ambient occlusion) military assault boat from a Battlefield-style browser shooter. Same look as the game's existing voxel jeep and tank: chunky readable shapes, olive woodland camouflage (olive #687341 ground with mid green #465a2e, olive-brown #5c4b30 and black-green #29361f blots), dark charcoal fittings, gunmetal guns, and saturated team recognition panels. Show the WEST team boat (woodland camo, cobalt blue #2a5ce6 recognition panels) as a 3/4 front hero view floating on a flat voxel river (teal-green water cubes, 24 m wide, grassy voxel banks with reeds), plus a small side view, a top-down view, and the EAST team version (sand desert camo #c9a066 with tan #a57a49 and dark khaki #6b5235 blots, signal orange #f0661c recognition panels) in an inset seen from 150 m. No text, no logos, no letters.

Variant B, rigid inflatable assault boat (RIB): an 8 m long, 2.8 m wide boat with thick dark rubber tube sponsons running round both sides and the bow, a camouflaged hard deck inside, a raised bow with a .50 heavy machine gun on a tall pintle post and a flat gun shield, the gunner standing exposed. Amidships a stand-up jockey console with a roll bar over it, a steering wheel and a small screen, the driver standing exposed behind it. Two jockey seats behind the console for exposed passengers, a rack of jerrycans and an ammo box, and two big black outboard motors on the transom. Recognition: the roll bar canopy top painted as a big cobalt square panel (visible from the air), the tube sponsons carrying a broad cobalt stripe along both flanks, and the outboard covers in cobalt. A small roundel on the console sides. White bow spray and a foamy twin wake.
