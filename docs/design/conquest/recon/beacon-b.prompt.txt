Game UI design mockup, 16:10, for "Voxel Blitz", a voxel (Minecraft-like blocky world) Battlefield-style Conquest shooter. Match the existing HUD exactly in style: dark translucent navy panels (#0d151b at ~90%), thin 1.5 px borders, condensed uppercase sans-serif labels (Rajdhani / Barlow Condensed, wide letter spacing), own team cyan-blue #4cc3ff, enemy orange #ff8a3d, squad green #7ef29a, spotted/hostile red #ff4b4b, selection highlight amber-gold #ffd166. Flat vector glyphs, no gradients, no glossy effects.
New feature to show: the RECON class gets two gadgets. Gadget 0 SPAWN BEACON: a small ground transmitter the Recon places; its squad can deploy on it (forward spawn), with a 10 s cooldown after each spawn, blocked while enemies are within 20 m. Gadget 1 MOTION SENSOR: a thrown puck that for 20 s reveals enemies moving faster than a crouch walk within 30 m, as red markers on the team's minimap.
