Use case: game level design concept sheet. Asset type: map design reference for a new arena in an existing browser voxel shooter (everything is built from visible 1 m cubes, flat matte colours, baked ambient occlusion in corners, no smooth geometry). The mode is a giant-mech team deathmatch: 8 versus 8 agile pilots who wallrun and double jump, each able to call down a 7 m tall walking battle mech from orbit, plus small AI infantry squads.
One wide image split into two panels, labelled LAYOUT C and ATMOSPHERE C.
Left panel LAYOUT C: an orthographic top-down tactical plan of a 224 x 176 m rectangular map, longer east-west. Theme: an industrial refinery town split by a dry canal. West edge: blue team yard; east edge: orange team yard. A dry concrete canal 20 m wide runs north-south through the centre, crossed by two broad mech-capable ramps and one narrow pilot footbridge. Each half has a big open refinery plaza with storage tanks (destructible mech cover), a tall pipe rack running east-west that pilots climb and run along, and a cluster of three- to five-storey worker tenements with narrow wallrun alleys and rooftop gaps. Wide streets loop around the plazas for mechs. The whole plan is point-symmetric. No text other than the two panel labels.
Right panel ATMOSPHERE C: a ground-level view in the same voxel style from a tenement rooftop down into the dry canal at sunset: green-grey toxic haze, rusty pipe racks, cracked concrete, sodium lamps, a mech wading up a ramp, pilots double jumping across the rooftops.
No logos, no real game names, no UI.
