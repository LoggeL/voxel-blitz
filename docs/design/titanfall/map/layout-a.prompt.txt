Use case: game level design concept sheet. Asset type: map design reference for a new arena in an existing browser voxel shooter (everything is built from visible 1 m cubes, flat matte colours, baked ambient occlusion in corners, no smooth geometry). The mode is a giant-mech team deathmatch: 8 versus 8 agile pilots who wallrun and double jump, each able to call down a 7 m tall walking battle mech from orbit, plus small AI infantry squads.
One wide image split into two panels, labelled LAYOUT A and ATMOSPHERE A.
Left panel LAYOUT A: an orthographic top-down tactical plan of a 224 x 176 m rectangular map, longer east-west. Theme: a ruined frontier mining colony at dusk. West edge: a blue team landing yard (open concrete apron with small blue-roofed spawn shelters). East edge: the mirrored orange team yard. Exact centre: a big open circular titan arena 60 m across around a collapsed smelter cooling tower ring (broken curved concrete wall segments 6 m high that give mech-height cover, with wide gaps). Three east-west lanes: the arena lane in the middle and two 14 m wide cracked asphalt boulevards near the north and south edges, all wide enough for mechs. Between the lanes: dense blocks of four- to six-storey colony buildings (habitat blocks, a refinery hall, a relay tower), separated by narrow 4 m wallrun alleys with long unbroken walls, rooftop routes with jumpable gaps, and catwalk bridges at 5 m height spanning the alleys so mechs cannot enter. North-south cross streets 12 m wide connect the lanes. Scattered low concrete barriers and shipping containers as destructible cover. The whole plan is point-symmetric (rotated 180 degrees about the centre). Mark lanes with faint arrows; no text other than the two panel labels.
Right panel ATMOSPHERE A: a ground-level view in the same voxel style from the arena edge looking across the broken cooling tower ring: warm orange dusk haze, a huge pale gas-giant planet low in the sky, rust-red corrugated steel, soot-dark brick, pale concrete habitat blocks with lit amber windows, a blue-camouflage voxel mech walking in the arena and a pilot wallrunning along a building wall. Drifting embers, long shadows.
No logos, no real game names, no UI.
