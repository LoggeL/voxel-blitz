Use case: game level design concept sheet. Asset type: map design reference for a new arena in an existing browser voxel shooter (everything is built from visible 1 m cubes, flat matte colours, baked ambient occlusion in corners, no smooth geometry). The mode is a giant-mech team deathmatch: 8 versus 8 agile pilots who wallrun and double jump, each able to call down a 7 m tall walking battle mech from orbit, plus small AI infantry squads.
One wide image split into two panels, labelled LAYOUT B and ATMOSPHERE B.
Left panel LAYOUT B: an orthographic top-down tactical plan of a 224 x 176 m rectangular map, longer east-west. Theme: a wrecked orbital spaceport. West and east edges: the two team staging aprons (blue west, orange east) with small spawn hangars. Centre: a huge cracked circular landing pad 64 m across with the broken hull of a crashed cargo dropship lying diagonally across it (the hull gives mech-height cover and pilots can run along its top). Around the pad: a ring road wide enough for mechs. North: a long multi-storey terminal building with a glass concourse that pilots run through (doorways too small for mechs) and a wide boulevard in front of it. South: rows of hangars and a control tower with long flat walls for wallrunning, separated by narrow pilot alleys and bridged by rooftop catwalks. Fuel tanks and cargo containers as destructible cover. The whole plan is point-symmetric. No text other than the two panel labels.
Right panel ATMOSPHERE B: a ground-level view in the same voxel style across the cracked landing pad toward the crashed dropship hull, pale cold morning light with blue-grey haze, white and teal hangar panels, yellow hazard stripes on the pad, an orange-camouflage voxel mech firing at a pilot on the hull. Drifting dust.
No logos, no real game names, no UI.
