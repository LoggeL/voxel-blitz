Use case: game level design concept art for an EXISTING browser voxel shooter map. Keep the existing art style exactly: everything is built from visible 1 m cubes with flat matte colours and baked ambient occlusion; no smooth geometry, no photoreal textures. The current map "Ashfall" (seen in the game's own renders) is a ruined smelter colony at dusk inside a red canyon: four- and five-storey blocks of pale concrete with pale stripe bands, soot-dark brick walk-ups, rust-brown corrugated steel halls with sawtooth roofs, teal- and yellow-trimmed admin blocks, dark asphalt lanes 14 m wide with pale dashed centre lines, a broken voxel cooling-tower ring of pale concrete in the middle with jagged stepped tops, blue team yard in the west and orange team yard in the east, a jagged red rock canyon rim round the map, a peach-orange dusk sky with a violet zenith, warm lanterns. The mode is giant-mech team deathmatch: agile pilots who wallrun along walls, double jump and ride enemy mechs (rodeo), and 7 m tall walking mechs (titans) that need wide lanes. The current map is too flat and empty: lanes and the arena are open asphalt, the pilots have no rooftop flow, nothing is memorable.
Concept C, "Terraces and Caches": one wide image with two panels labelled C1 and C2.
C1 (elevated view of one half of the map): the colony is built on two levels: raised concrete loading terraces 3 m high with wide titan ramps front the arena, with chest-high parapets that give mechs hull-down positions; between the habitat blocks a rooftop circuit of skybridges, ladders and plank crossings links every roof and the team yard watchtowers; tall freestanding billboard panels hang over the cross streets above mech height for pilots to wallrun across the street.
C2 (close view of a pilot nook): a small lit alcove under a low skybridge in a 4 m alley, glowing green battery cell on a charging cradle, lit hazard signs, cables, a pilot crouching beside it; through the alley mouth a mech's legs pass by.
