Use case: game level design concept art for an EXISTING browser voxel shooter map. Keep the existing art style exactly: everything is built from visible 1 m cubes with flat matte colours and baked ambient occlusion; no smooth geometry, no photoreal textures. The current map "Ashfall" (seen in the game's own renders) is a ruined smelter colony at dusk inside a red canyon: four- and five-storey blocks of pale concrete with pale stripe bands, soot-dark brick walk-ups, rust-brown corrugated steel halls with sawtooth roofs, teal- and yellow-trimmed admin blocks, dark asphalt lanes 14 m wide with pale dashed centre lines, a broken voxel cooling-tower ring of pale concrete in the middle with jagged stepped tops, blue team yard in the west and orange team yard in the east, a jagged red rock canyon rim round the map, a peach-orange dusk sky with a violet zenith, warm lanterns. The mode is giant-mech team deathmatch: agile pilots who wallrun along walls, double jump and ride enemy mechs (rodeo), and 7 m tall walking mechs (titans) that need wide lanes. The current map is too flat and empty: lanes and the arena are open asphalt, the pilots have no rooftop flow, nothing is memorable.
Concept A, "The Pour": one wide image with two panels labelled A1 and A2.
A1 (elevated three-quarter view over the central arena): the broken cooling-tower ring now holds a glowing crucible pool of molten orange slag in its centre, fed by a stream of slag pouring from a tipped steel ladle that hangs under a long steel pipe-rack catwalk crossing the whole arena north to south 11 m above the ground on slim lattice legs (titans walk under it, pilots run across it behind low pipe bundles). Two narrow glowing slag runner channels cut from the crucible to the ring wall. Inside and around the ring: titan-height cover (stacked steel ingots, a tipped crucible, a fallen ring segment). Strong orange underglow from the slag on the concrete, smoke and embers rising.
A2 (pilot's-eye view in an approach yard next to the arena): a zig-zag chain of tall thin concrete blast walls 8 m high, staggered 5 m apart, that a pilot wallruns from one to the next; a raised concrete ore dock 3 m high with a wide stepped ramp where a blue voxel mech stands overlooking the arena; container stacks with a steel stair to a perch at mech-back height beside the lane.
No logos, no real game names, no UI, no text except the panel labels.
