Asset type: first-person cockpit interior design sheet for an existing browser voxel shooter, original art, no real game branding, no logos, no text other than the small panel labels listed. The pilot sits inside a fully CLOSED armoured box: roof panels, side walls, floor and footwell are all visible at the edges of a very wide field of view; the outside world is only visible through the one armoured viewport in front. The centre of the view stays completely clear for a crosshair; the four screen corners hold plain dark plating because the game draws its own HUD panels there (top-left weapon, top-right core gauge, bottom-right ability tiles), so detailed screens sit low in the console, left and centre. The sheet shows three cockpit variants in the same design language, labelled ATLAS (balanced medium frame), OGRE (heavy frame) and STRYDER (light frame): ATLAS large on top as a 16:9 first-person view, OGRE and STRYDER smaller side by side underneath, each also a 16:9 first-person view looking out at the voxel battlefield.
