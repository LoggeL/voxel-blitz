Use case: stylized storyboard concept for an existing browser voxel shooter, original art, no branding, no franchise art. A 2x2 grid of four 16:9 FIRST-PERSON frames showing a pilot EMBARKING into a giant blocky mech (titan). Style: everything built from visible cubes and chamfered low-poly boxes, flat shading, pale blue sky, green voxel grass, gunmetal grey mech armour (#343a42 to #5d6671) with yellow-black hazard stripes and cyan accent lights (#40c8ff). Frame 1: the kneeling titan's huge blocky left hand opens in front of the camera, palm up, the open chest hatch visible above with the dark cockpit cavity glowing cyan. Frame 2: the camera is lifted on the palm, the titan's chest and open hatch rush toward the camera, slight motion blur, the world tilting. Frame 3: inside the cabin looking out; thick armour hatch plates slide shut from top and bottom like heavy blast doors, the viewport octagon is half covered, a narrowing band of daylight, sparks of the locking bolts. Frame 4: hatch closed, the octagonal viewport glass reveals the world again, the console screens boot up one after another with cyan scanlines and a large projected boot text on the glass reading LINK ESTABLISHED and PILOT ONLINE, hull bars filling up. Thin white panel borders between frames, numbers 1 to 4 in the corners.
