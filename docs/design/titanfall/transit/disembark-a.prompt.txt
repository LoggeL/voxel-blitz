Use case: stylized storyboard concept for an existing browser voxel shooter, original art, no branding, no franchise art. A 2x2 grid of four 16:9 frames showing a pilot DISEMBARKING from a giant blocky mech (titan). Style: everything built from visible cubes and chamfered low-poly boxes, flat shading, pale blue sky, green voxel grass, gunmetal grey mech armour (#343a42 to #5d6671) with yellow-black hazard stripes and cyan accent lights (#40c8ff). Frame 1 (first person, inside the cockpit): the console screens go dark, a projected text on the glass reads HATCH OPEN, steam vents hiss from the ring edges. Frame 2 (first person): the armour hatch splits and swings up and away, bright daylight floods the cabin, the camera rises out of the seat toward the opening. Frame 3 (third person, side view): a small blocky soldier vaults out of the titan's open chest hatch in an arc, white vent steam behind, the titan kneeling. Frame 4 (first person, on the ground): the pilot lands in a crouch in front of the titan's feet with a small dust puff, a gun in hand, the titan standing up behind as an AUTO-TITAN with its cyan eye light. Thin white panel borders between frames, numbers 1 to 4 in the corners.
