Use case: storyboard keyframes for a UI/camera animation in an existing browser voxel shooter, original art, no branding, no franchise logos. A 2x2 grid of four 16:9 FIRST-PERSON frames from inside the cockpit of a giant blocky mech (titan), the moment right after the pilot sits down. Existing cockpit to keep in every frame: a wide octagonal viewport in a thick gunmetal strut ring (#4a525c, #5d6671) with yellow-black hazard stripe bands above and below, rows of hex bolts, four angled dark screen pods in the corners (top left WEAPON "SPLITTER RIFLE 20/22", top right CORE "46%" ring, bottom left DASH/HULL bars, bottom right ability tiles READY), a brow display reading ATTRITION at the top, an MFD console deck at the bottom with small cyan/green screens. Outside: flat green voxel grass field, pale blue sky. Flat shading, everything built from chamfered low-poly boxes, cyan accent light #40c8ff. Thin white borders between frames, numbers 1 to 4 in the corners.
VARIANT B, "voxel shutter mosaic":
Frame 1: two angular armour plates sweep in FROM THE LEFT like scissor blades (upper and lower, hinged at the left pillar, rotating closed), dark gunmetal with hazard edges; outside daylight still visible in a wedge.
Frame 2: one big wedge-shaped plate slides in FROM THE RIGHT and meets them, forming a closed chevron pattern; red warning lamps blink, the cabin is dark.
Frame 3: locked: a ring of tiny cyan lock lights along the seams turns from red to cyan, a small jolt.
Frame 4: the closed hatch's inner face becomes a display: the outside view assembles from big square voxel pixel blocks that resolve from coarse to sharp, left to right, with a cyan scan bar; text "LINK ESTABLISHED / PILOT ONLINE"; screens booting.
