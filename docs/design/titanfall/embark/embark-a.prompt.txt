Use case: storyboard keyframes for a UI/camera animation in an existing browser voxel shooter, original art, no branding, no franchise logos. A 2x2 grid of four 16:9 FIRST-PERSON frames from inside the cockpit of a giant blocky mech (titan), the moment right after the pilot sits down. Existing cockpit to keep in every frame: a wide octagonal viewport in a thick gunmetal strut ring (#4a525c, #5d6671) with yellow-black hazard stripe bands above and below, rows of hex bolts, four angled dark screen pods in the corners (top left WEAPON "SPLITTER RIFLE 20/22", top right CORE "46%" ring, bottom left DASH/HULL bars, bottom right ability tiles READY), a brow display reading ATTRITION at the top, an MFD console deck at the bottom with small cyan/green screens. Outside: flat green voxel grass field, pale blue sky. Flat shading, everything built from chamfered low-poly boxes, cyan accent light #40c8ff. Thin white borders between frames, numbers 1 to 4 in the corners.
VARIANT A, "sliding armour plates then CRT power-on":
Frame 1: the cockpit is unlit (screens dark, only red warning lamps), outside daylight still visible through the viewport; two heavy armour plates slide in horizontally FROM THE LEFT one after the other (the upper one already half across, the lower one just entering), each plate dark gunmetal with ribs, bolts and a yellow-black hazard edge on its leading side.
Frame 2: the two left plates are home and a single large plate slides in FROM THE RIGHT with a slanted leading edge, closing the remaining gap; a narrow vertical slit of daylight remains along the slanted seam.
Frame 3: all three plates interlock along a slanted seam and a horizontal seam (a Y-shaped seam), small sparks and a bright cyan flash along the seams as the locks bang home, camera slightly shaken, cabin almost black.
Frame 4: the inside of the closed hatch is a SCREEN that powers on like an old monitor: a bright cyan-white horizontal line across the middle expands vertically into the outside camera feed (the green field and sky) with faint scanlines and a cyan tint, a projected text on the glass "NEURAL LINK", the corner pods flickering on one after another.
