Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: design reference for diegetic on-gun ammo displays (like the ammo counter on top of a science-fiction assault rifle) for the pilot weapons of a giant-mech team deathmatch mode in an existing browser voxel shooter. One wide image, labelled OPTION B - REAR DECK COUNTER, with one large desktop 16:9 first-person view on the left two thirds and a column of four small close-up insets on the right.
The game world and gun style to match exactly (taken from a real first-person capture of the current build): a browser voxel shooter, sunset sky in peach and lilac with blocky purple voxel mountains, an ochre and brown voxel prairie, a dark asphalt road with white dashes, blocky dark green voxel pine trees, small voxel houses with red roofs. First-person hip view: the gun sits in the lower right quarter seen from slightly above and behind its left side, held by two dark green padded armoured gloves; the guns are chunky low-poly box models, matte near-black gunmetal and polymer with flat colour accents, no fine detail. Pilot HUD around the edges, unchanged: top centre attrition score bar MILITIA 142 / 08:41 / 128 IMC on charcoal glass, bottom left hexagonal TITAN 46% gauge and INTEGRITY 100 health bar, small Q GRAPPLE chip with two amber pips right of the screen centre, bottom right ANTI-TITAN chip with a thin ring and a grenade chip M-4 FRAG x2. The stock floating ammo card at the bottom right (big 30 / 90 with a weapon icon) is GONE: the ammo now lives on the gun. Typography on the screens: condensed uppercase Rajdhani-style digits, wide letter spacing, tabular figures. Colours per weapon from the game: CR-6 LONGBOW red-orange #ff5a3c, AT-12 ARCHER cyan #4ee6ff, VK-77 RAPTOR rifle amber #ffa03c, KR-50 KRABER gold #ffc04a, TB-4 THUNDERBOLT blue #7aa8ff, HORNET SMG cyan #59e8ff; low ammo turns the digits warning red #ff3355; RELOADING blinks in white over a progress bar. Only these labels, no logos, no real brands, no extra stats.
OPTION B design: a small square counter screen on the TOP REAR of the receiver, just behind the sights, tilted back about 50 degrees toward the shooter's eye so it is readable both from the hip and while aiming down sights (it sits below the sight picture). Layout: very large two-digit magazine count centred, the reserve small underneath, a thin arc of segments around the digits that drains with the magazine, a weapon-coloured glow halo on the housing. Main view: CR-6 LONGBOW charge rifle with the counter reading 3 and reserve 9, the arc around it doubling as a charge ring at 64%.
Insets: 1) AT-12 ARCHER: counter reads 1, reserve 4, a cyan lock arc closing at 55% with LOCK text; 2) aim-down-sights view of the VK-77 RAPTOR rifle, the counter visible at the bottom of the sight picture reading 30 / 90 in amber; 3) low ammo 4 in warning red; 4) RELOADING state with the arc filling as progress.
