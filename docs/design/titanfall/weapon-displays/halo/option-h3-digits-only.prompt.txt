Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: design reference for a tiny diegetic on-gun ammo counter in the spirit of the Halo assault rifle counter (Halo Reach style), for the pilot weapons of a giant-mech team deathmatch mode in an existing browser voxel shooter. One wide image, labelled OPTION H3 - MINIMAL GLOW DIGITS, with one large desktop 16:9 first-person view on the left two thirds and a column of four small close-up insets on the right.
The game world and gun style to match exactly (taken from a real first-person capture of the current build): a browser voxel shooter, sunset sky in peach and lilac with blocky purple voxel mountains, an ochre and brown voxel prairie, a dark asphalt road with white dashes, blocky dark green voxel pine trees, small voxel houses with red roofs. First-person hip view: the gun sits in the lower right quarter seen from slightly above and behind, the barrel pointing away from the viewer into the scene, held by two dark green padded armoured gloves; the guns are chunky low-poly box models, matte near-black gunmetal and polymer with flat colour accents, no fine detail. Pilot HUD around the edges, unchanged: top centre attrition score bar MILITIA 142 / 08:41 / 128 IMC on charcoal glass, bottom left hexagonal TITAN 46% gauge and INTEGRITY 100 health bar, small Q GRAPPLE chip with two amber pips right of the screen centre, bottom right ANTI-TITAN chip with a thin ring and a grenade chip M-4 FRAG x2. There is NO ammo number anywhere on the HUD: the old floating ammo card at the bottom right (30 / 90 with a weapon icon) is completely removed, the bottom right corner holds only the ANTI-TITAN and grenade chips. There is NO screen or panel on the side or flank of the gun. Typography of the digits: condensed uppercase Rajdhani-style digits, tabular figures. Colours per weapon from the game: VK-77 RAPTOR rifle amber #ffa03c, CR-6 LONGBOW charge rifle red-orange #ff5a3c; low ammo turns the digits warning red #ff3355; RELOADING blinks in white. Only these labels, no logos, no real brands, no extra stats.
OPTION H3 design, the smallest and most compact possible: no visible screen frame or bezel. On the TOP REAR of the receiver, directly in front of the shooter's eye and just behind the rear sight, there is a tiny dark smoked-glass window, about the size of a thumbnail, flush with the receiver and angled back toward the eye so it is read from behind, looking along the barrel direction - exactly like the Halo assault rifle round counter. Inside it: two large glowing weapon-coloured digits (the magazine count) that fill most of the window, surrounded by a thin ring of short glowing segments that drains with the magazine (or fills as charge progress). Directly under the digits a tiny dim number shows the reserve. Faint soft glow of the digits on the surrounding gunmetal. Nothing else: no labels, no icons, no bars on the gun.
Main view: VK-77 RAPTOR rifle from the hip, the tiny rear window on top of the receiver clearly readable from the shooter's viewpoint, reading big amber 30 with the full segment ring and a tiny dim 90 underneath.
Insets (each a close-up from behind the gun looking along the barrel): 1) aim-down-sights view of the VK-77 RAPTOR: the iron/holo sight centred, the tiny amber 30 counter window visible just below and behind the sight picture at the bottom of the view; 2) CR-6 LONGBOW charge rifle in red-orange, counter reading 3 with reserve 9, the segment ring half-filled and glowing brighter as a charge ring at 64%; 3) low ammo: VK-77 counter reading 5 in warning red #ff3355 with only a few ring segments left; 4) RELOADING: the digits replaced by a blinking white RELOADING-style dash pair, the segment ring refilling clockwise as progress.
