Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: design reference for a diegetic on-gun ammo counter for the pilot weapons of a giant-mech team deathmatch mode in an existing browser voxel shooter, inspired by the classic science-fiction assault rifle whose ammo counter sits on top of the receiver and is read from behind. One wide image, labelled OPTION H1 - HALO REAR DECK COUNTER, with one large desktop 16:9 first-person view on the left two thirds and a column of four small close-up insets on the right.
The game world and gun style to match exactly (taken from a real first-person capture of the current build): a browser voxel shooter, sunset sky in peach and lilac with blocky purple voxel mountains, an ochre and brown voxel prairie, a dark asphalt road with white dashes, blocky dark green voxel pine trees, small voxel houses with red roofs. First-person hip view: the gun sits in the lower right quarter, seen from directly behind and slightly above, the barrel pointing forward into the scene, held by two dark green padded armoured gloves; the guns are chunky low-poly box models, matte near-black gunmetal and polymer with flat colour accents, no fine detail. Pilot HUD around the edges, unchanged: top centre attrition score bar MILITIA 142 / 08:41 / 128 IMC on charcoal glass, bottom left hexagonal TITAN 46% gauge and INTEGRITY 100 health bar, small Q GRAPPLE chip with two amber pips right of the screen centre, bottom right ANTI-TITAN chip with a thin ring and a grenade chip M-4 FRAG x2. There is NO ammo number anywhere on the HUD: the stock floating ammo card at the bottom right (big 30 / 90 with a weapon icon) is completely GONE, and there is NO display panel on the side or flank of the gun. The only ammo readout in the whole image is the small counter on the top rear of each gun. Typography on the screens: condensed uppercase Rajdhani-style seven-segment digits, tabular figures. Colours per weapon from the game: VK-77 RAPTOR rifle amber #ffa03c, CR-6 LONGBOW charge rifle red-orange #ff5a3c; low ammo turns the digits warning red #ff3355; RELOADING blinks in white.
OPTION H1 design: a COMPACT counter, much smaller than a palm, only about 45 mm wide and 30 mm tall, a small rectangular backlit screen recessed into the TOP REAR of the receiver directly behind and below the rear sight. The screen face points BACKWARD toward the shooter's eye, along the barrel axis, tilted up about 35 degrees, so the player reads it looking forward down the gun, exactly like the ammo counter on top of a sci-fi assault rifle. It is framed by a thin dark bezel flush with the receiver, no bracket, no external box. Layout: mostly one very large bold two-digit magazine count filling the screen; a thin ring or arc of small segments around the digits that drains as the magazine empties; the reserve as tiny digits underneath. A soft weapon-tinted glow spills onto the receiver top. Readable from the hip and visible just below the sight picture when aiming.
Main view: VK-77 RAPTOR assault rifle from the hip, the rear deck counter reading 30 in amber with a full amber segment ring and tiny reserve 90 below.
Insets (each a close-up from behind the gun, same style): 1) aim-down-sights view of the VK-77 RAPTOR: the iron sight / small reflex sight centred, the counter glowing amber just below the sight picture at the bottom of the frame reading 30 / 90; 2) CR-6 LONGBOW charge rifle counter in red-orange reading 3 with reserve 9, the ring filling as a charge meter at 64%; 3) low ammo: the counter reads 05 in warning red #ff3355 with only a few red segments left; 4) RELOADING state: the digits replaced by a white RELOADING word over the ring filling as progress. Small caption under each inset: ADS, LONGBOW CHARGE, LOW AMMO, RELOADING. Only these labels, no logos, no real brands, no extra stats.
