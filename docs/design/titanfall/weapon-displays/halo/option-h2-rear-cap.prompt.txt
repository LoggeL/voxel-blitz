Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: design reference for a compact diegetic on-gun ammo counter in the spirit of the Halo 3 MA5C assault rifle counter, for the pilot weapons of a giant-mech team deathmatch mode in an existing browser voxel shooter. One wide image, labelled OPTION H2 - REAR END-CAP SCREEN, with one large desktop 16:9 first-person view on the left two thirds and a column of four small close-up insets on the right.
The game world and gun style to match exactly (taken from a real first-person capture of the current build): a browser voxel shooter, sunset sky in peach and lilac with blocky purple voxel mountains, an ochre and brown voxel prairie, a dark asphalt road with white dashes, blocky dark green voxel pine trees, small voxel houses with red roofs. First-person hip view: the gun sits in the lower right quarter, seen from BEHIND and slightly above, the barrel pointing away from the viewer into the scene, held by two dark green padded armoured gloves; the guns are chunky low-poly box models, matte near-black gunmetal and polymer with flat colour accents, no fine detail. Pilot HUD around the edges: top centre attrition score bar MILITIA 142 / 08:41 / 128 IMC on charcoal glass, bottom left hexagonal TITAN 46% gauge and INTEGRITY 100 health bar, small Q GRAPPLE chip with two amber pips right of the screen centre, bottom right ANTI-TITAN chip with a thin ring and a grenade chip M-4 FRAG x2. There is NO ammo number anywhere on the HUD: no floating ammo card, no 30 / 90 text in the screen corner. The ammo lives only on the gun. There is NO screen or panel on the side or flank of the gun: the left and right sides of the receiver are plain dark metal.
Typography on the gun screen: condensed uppercase Rajdhani-style digits, tabular figures, glowing emissive LCD look. Colours per weapon from the game: VK-77 RAPTOR rifle amber #ffa03c, CR-6 LONGBOW red-orange #ff5a3c; low ammo turns the digits warning red #ff3355; RELOADING in white. Only these labels, no logos, no real brands, no extra stats.
OPTION H2 design (Halo-inspired, minimal, COMPACT): a small square screen built into the REAR FACE of the receiver, at the back end of the optic housing on top of the gun, flat and vertical, facing straight back along the weapon's direction toward the shooter's eye, so the player reads it looking down the length of the gun exactly like the Halo 3 assault rifle counter. The screen is roughly the size of a matchbox, framed by a chunky thick dark bezel that is part of the gun body (moulded, not a stuck-on tablet). Screen content is only: one huge two-digit magazine count filling most of the screen, a single thin horizontal bar directly beneath it (magazine fill, the same bar doubles as charge / reload progress), and the reserve as a tiny number in the top right corner of the screen. Dark background, glowing weapon-coloured digits, a faint matching glow spill on the bezel. Nothing else on the gun is lit.
Main view: VK-77 RAPTOR assault rifle from behind at hip, the rear end-cap screen clearly visible and readable reading 30 in amber, the bar full, reserve 90 small in the corner.
Insets: 1) aim-down-sights view of the VK-77 RAPTOR: the sight picture and reticle in the upper part, the rear end-cap counter directly below/behind the sight, facing the eye, reading 30 in amber; 2) CR-6 LONGBOW charge rifle rear end-cap screen reading 3 in red-orange, reserve 9 in the corner, the bar under the digit filling as charge at 64%; 3) low ammo: the counter reads 5 in warning red #ff3355 with the bar almost empty; 4) RELOADING: the screen shows the word RELOADING in white with the thin bar filling as progress.
