# RX-8 HAVOC — design alternatives (revision 2 redo)

The repo workflow asks for design alternatives before a substantial visual
change. No image generator is available in this toolset, so each alternative
below was generated as a real-geometry Blender blockout by
`concept-blockouts.py` (flat palette colours, coarse forms) and rendered to
`concept-{1..3}-*.png`. The prompt text is kept verbatim per alternative — it
is the brief each blockout was generated from, and the same text would drive an
image generator if one were used.

Shared frame for all three prompts:

> Heavy sci-fi shoulder-fired rocket launcher for a first-person shooter
> viewmodel, display name RX-8 HAVOC. Overall length about 1 m, launch tube
> diameter 12 cm, warm industrial palette: gunmetal, olive drab, orange safety
> paint, ivory markings, dark polymer grips, black rubber. The forward tube
> half is a bare heat sleeve (a support hand grips there) and must stay free of
> furniture; a fat warhead sits visibly at the muzzle. Flip-up ladder sights
> bracket a clear sight line along the top. Squared or cage-braced structure,
> industrial panels and handles, a convincing pistol grip and shoulder rest,
> a visibly separated breech with a tilting back-blast venturi tail. Product
> render on a neutral studio backdrop, 3/4 front-right hero angle. NOT a thin
> smooth disposable AT4 tube, NO flared orange funnel cone.

## Alternative 1 — "ANVIL" (squared armour receiver)

Prompt delta: *Give the launcher a heavy squared receiver: an octagonal
armoured shell cladding the rear tube half, bolted side access panels with
orange bolt bosses, ivory top panel, long slab handle rails along the flanks.
Chunky machined blocks, chamfered corners, mass concentrated at the rear. The
warhead is a fat ivory ogive on a slim boom protruding from the muzzle collar,
with a wide orange identification band.*

Silhouette: heavy rear mass, industrial slab, strong horizontal panel breaks.

## Alternative 2 — "CAGE" (open exoskeleton lattice)

Prompt delta: *Replace the receiver cladding with an open cage-braced
exoskeleton: four corner longerons, three square hoops and orange diagonal
cross-braces around the bare launch tube, the tube visible through the
lattice. Skeletal, mechanical, light. The warhead is a long slim olive dart
with four wrap lugs, protruding well clear of the muzzle.*

Silhouette: skeletal, technical, maximum negative space.

## Alternative 3 — "BULWARK" (hybrid squared breech + half cage)

Prompt delta: *Hybrid: a squared olive breech housing with grey cheek plates
directly behind a short half-cage of diagonal braces, prominent tubular carry
handles, ivory spine deck. Big orange warhead ogive seated in a forked muzzle
bracket. Aggressive forward-leaning mass, the warhead cradled like heavy
ordnance ready to launch.*

Silhouette: dense mid mass with a cradled warhead nose, most aggressive read.

## Choice

**Chosen: alternative 3, BULWARK** — recorded with the reasoning and the two
refinements that came with it (warhead finish from ANVIL's palette; the proud
warhead tightened to the runtime owner's seated-nose spec) in
`../build-report.md`.
