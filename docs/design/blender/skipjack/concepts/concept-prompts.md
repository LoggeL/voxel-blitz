# SKIPJACK concept exploration

Generated first with the built-in ImageGen tool. Concept B was selected: its
three-round hinged rotary cassette gives the weapon a strong silhouette and
matches the planned three-round gameplay magazine. The Blender model is rebuilt
from its major forms and materials; the concept image is a design reference,
not a runtime texture.

```text

Primary request: design a compact, premium-quality sci-fi grenade launcher for a voxel arena shooter. Concept A: six-round rotary drum beneath a short 40 mm launch tube, with the six rounds clearly visible through an open protective cage.
Subject: a hand-held grenade launcher with a pistol grip and forward support grip, a sturdy receiver, one short bore, compact reflex sight, tactile reload latch, visible casing seams, exposed fasteners, crisp machined edges, restrained wear, clear 3D game-model silhouette.
Style/medium: polished realistic 3D game prop, detailed industrial design, high contrast but readable at first-person scale.
Composition/framing: one isolated three-quarter product view from slightly above and front, full weapon visible, neutral studio background, no hands, no scene clutter.
Lighting/mood: clean studio key, soft rim light, controlled metal reflections.
Color palette: charcoal gunmetal, muted olive coating, black rubber, a few safety-orange markers and small phosphor-green status lights.
Materials/textures: bead-blasted steel, hard anodized surfaces, molded grip panels, brass chamber rims, visible 40 mm cartridge tips.
Constraints: credible hand-held proportions; six loaded rounds; one barrel; weapon only.
Avoid: logos, readable text, people, gameplay HUD, muzzle flash, smoke, firing scene, disassembled parts.
```
