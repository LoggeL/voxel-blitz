# SKIPJACK rounded redesign, revision 11

Both concepts used `skipjack-hero.png` from revision 10 as an image reference. The
generated images are shape references; the authored Blender model and browser
captures are the evidence for the shipped geometry.

## Direction A

Prompt: Create a polished industrial design concept sheet for the attached
fictional videogame grenade launcher, preserving its recognizable compact
proportions, muzzle direction, olive and charcoal palette, side mounted
vertical three cell ammunition cage, and visible remaining shells. Redesign
the silhouette to be decisively smoother: flowing forged oval receiver,
sculpted polymer stock with continuous rounded shoulder, large radiused
transitions, smoothly cylindrical barrel jacket with detailed machined
surfaces, compact low arched reflex sight. The highest ammunition cell is
visibly empty because its round is already loaded internally; only the lower
two spare rounds show. Three consistent orthographic style views in one sheet:
left side, right three-quarter, top. Physically plausible hard surface form
that can be rebuilt in Blender; sophisticated AAA game weapon detail, fine
surface texture, PBR wear, subtle olive paint microtexture. Clean neutral
studio background, no labels, no text, no hands, no firing effects, no fantasy
spikes. Avoid large triangular fins and boxy slabs.

Output: `rounded-direction-a.png`.

## Direction B, selected

Prompt: Using the attached grenade launcher as dimensional and mechanical
reference, make a distinct second concept sheet for a much more elegant,
rounded redesign. Keep the broad compact silhouette and olive/graphite
identity, three-position external ammunition holder with empty top position
and two visible spare cartridges, horizontal barrel, rear stock, angled
pistol grip, small top sight. Make the main shell a continuously curved oval
clamshell, smooth monocoque stock with large concave thumb sweep, softened
ergonomic grips, cylindrical shroud with carefully machined circular ridges
and slots. Premium tangible hard surface design, weathered painted metal with
micro-scratches and subtle polymer grain, detailed normal-map-friendly seams
and fasteners. Show large left-side profile and two smaller consistent 3/4 and
rear views, no text, no annotations, neutral gray studio. Avoid all polygonal
slabs, sharp wedge shoulders, huge triangular sights, and rectangular stock
pads.

Output: `rounded-direction-b.png`. Selected for the single smooth receiver,
rounded stock, compact optic and circular barrel shroud. Gameplay anchors and
the existing cassette hinge take precedence over the concept proportions.
