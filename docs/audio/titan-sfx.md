# Titan weapon sound effects

The five primary weapons use a recorded, layered sound bank in
`public/assets/audio/titan/{splitter,t203,railgun,leadwall,predator,common}/`. TitanSynth remains the
fallback for missing reports, impacts and charge or spin loops. Gemini receives the audio itself
and judges its fit, punch, cleanliness, length and tail, and identity. Results and output hashes are
in [titan-weapons-judging.md](titan-weapons-judging.md).

The bank contains 58 selected files. The files are mono Ogg Opus at 48 kHz and 96 kbit/s, encoded with ffmpeg libopus and bitexact flags.
The local ffmpeg has no libvorbis. Every recording has a source URL, author, individually checked
licence, download hash, cut, processing chain and output hash in `titan/sources.json`. Every source,
including CC0, is credited in `titan/CREDITS.txt` and `public/assets/audio/LICENSES.md`.
No human audition or audible in-game playback was performed during this unattended pass.

## Bank

- Splitter: a short electric plasma crack with a tight low-mid pulse. The split cue layers three
  staggered plasma discharges. Its tails shimmer, and its impacts keep the ion chirp above the metal.
- T-203: a hollow grenade-launcher thump with its mortar body aligned to the attack, followed by the
  breech clank. Explosive impacts carry flare crackle, sizzle and a separate fading burn layer.
- Railgun: a 1.6 s rising charge, a full-charge hold loop and a ready cue. A real cannon crack and
  TAD's CC0 plasma discharge make the full shot; its rolling tail retains electric texture above
  the rumble. Tap-shot gain, pitch and visual kick follow the server event's charge.
- Leadwall: broad ballistic shotgun blasts, dense short pellet hits and clean armour ricochets.
  The pitched rack is compressed in time with ffmpeg atempo, preserving its pitch while completing
  before the next shot. The shell-carrier reload is stretched to the authoritative reload duration.
- Predator: tight autocannon rounds with short low-mid weight, steel feeder ticks and bounded overlap.
  The barrel motor carries torque, rotation pulses and bearing clicks instead of a drill whine.
  Bright brass bounces and a burst-ending tail accompany the stream. Its impacts have a separate
  kinetic crack and armour crunch bank.
- Common: short metallic hit ticks, one heavy doom cue, and a separate double-strike destruction cue.

Every group has variations where useful. Groups with multiple takes avoid repeating the preceding take. Single foundations vary playback pitch.
Selection uses later decoded variants even if the first download fails. Group mastering checks loudness after
Opus encoding and attenuates louder takes from the original float render.

## In-game wiring

`TitanSynth` (`public/js/vehicles/vehicle-audio.js`) plays the groups.
`titan-presentation.js` `init()` loads the bank with
`VehicleAudio.loadTitanBank(TITAN_SAMPLE_MANIFEST)` when the Titanfall client starts, and `dispose()` unloads
it. Failed slots are retried after 5 s and 20 s. Until a group decodes, its previous synthesized voice
plays, so the game never goes silent. Levels are set in `TITAN_WEAPON_MIX` and limits in `TITAN_AUDIO`.

| Cue | Groups | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Shot | `fire` + `tail` (all weapons), `split` | Every primary `shoot` event (`titan-presentation.js` → `titanShot`). The ION split layer plays when the event has more than one path. |
| Mechanics | `t203.mech`, `leadwall.pump` | 0.5-0.58 s / 0.10-0.11 s after the shot, own titan or within 45 m. |
| Railgun | `charge`, `ready`, `hold` | Mount field 6 of the presented row. The charge take is stretched to the server's 1.6 s and starts at the charge already reached. At full charge the ready cue plays and the hold loop runs until the shot. The report's gain and pitch scale with `event.charge`. |
| Predator | `fire`, `casings`, `tail`, `spinUp`, `spin`, `spinDown` | One round per shot, with at most 5 overlapping. Brass plays every 4th round (own titan or within 40 m). The tail plays 0.16 s after the burst stops (bursts of 3 rounds or more). The spin-up is synced to the server's 0.6 s. The loop holds full spin, and the spin-down follows the barrels down. |
| Reload | `splitter/leadwall/predator.reload` | The own magazine reload starting (mount field 5 rises from 0). Playback rate follows the server reload duration; the remaining fraction sets the starting offset. |
| Impact | `impact`, `t203.burn` | Hitscan rounds that stopped short of their range, T-203 shells, and Leadwall pellet hits with radius > 0 (`titan-projectile-views.js`). Pellet hits are capped at 3 per 0.12 s. |
| Ricochet | `leadwall.ricochet` | `projectileUpdate` of a tracked pellet, capped at 2 per 0.15 s. |
| Confirm | `hit.confirm`, `hit.doom`, `hit.kill` | Own effective `vehicle_hit` while piloting a titan (at most one per 0.07 s), `titan_doomed` by the own rounds, and `vehicle_destroyed` credited to the pilot. Doom and destruction have distinct glass labels. |

- **Own titan:** plays in the head at `selfWeaponGain` 0.8.
- **Other titans:** positioned with HRTF, culled at 280 m (impacts at 90 m, ricochets at 60 m). Past 60 m a low-pass falls from 16 kHz to 2.4 kHz at 280 m, and the tail's share grows with distance. The charge whine and the barrel spin are heard within 55 m.
- **Voice budget:** weapon voices have their own budget of 20, so a Predator burst never starves footfalls or titan cues.
- **Frag fallback removed:** `sfx.explosion` returns early for `t203` and `leadwall`, so a Leadwall shot is no longer ten frag blasts.

## Build

`tools/titan-sfx/README.md` lists the steps. Fetch the sources into a work folder outside the repository,
`python3 -I tools/titan-sfx/build.py`, check with `sheet.py` and `judge_all.py`, then
`python3 -I tools/titan-sfx/integrate.py` to ship the bank and regenerate the tables above.
