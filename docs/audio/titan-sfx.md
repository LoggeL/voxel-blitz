# Titan weapon sound effects

The five titan primary weapons (ION Splitter Rifle, Scorch T-203, Northstar Plasma Railgun, Ronin
Leadwall, Legion Predator Cannon) and the titan hit confirms play recorded sound from the bank in
`public/assets/audio/titan/{splitter,t203,railgun,leadwall,predator,common}/`. The files replace the
synthesized TitanSynth voices, which stay as the fallback until a group has decoded.

`public/assets/audio/titan/sources.json` has the full provenance of every file. All files are mono Ogg
Opus at 48 kHz and 96 kbit/s, made with libopus (`-fflags +bitexact`). No Vorbis is used, because the
local ffmpeg has no libvorbis.

The sounds were checked with spectrograms, loudness, peak and loop-seam measurements, and judged by a
model that receives the audio (Google Gemini `gemini-3.8-flash`, see
[titan-weapons-judging.md](titan-weapons-judging.md)). **Nobody has listened to them in the game yet**, so
treat the playback gains as first estimates.

## Bank

Every file is one take of a group in `TITAN_SAMPLE_GROUPS` (`public/js/audio/titan-bank.js`). Takes of a
group are levelled together, so any take can play in place of another.

- **Splitter**: `fire` (4 takes, 0.28 s) is a real rifle crack with a pitched-down body copy, a
  plasma-rifle layer and an electric zap. `split` (2, 0.62 s) is heavier: a heavy blaster and a laser blast over a
  rifle pitched down to ~0.75x. `tail` (2) is forest reverb from a rifle recording, cut after the shot.
  `impact` (3) layers energy hits, welding sparks and metal. `reload` is a launcher latch (cell out), a
  charge-up whine and a shell-load click (cell lock).
- **T-203**: `fire` (3) is an M203 / grenade launcher / Mk 19 round pitched down to ~0.8x over a mortar
  launch body, a sub thump and a short fire whoosh. `tail` is mortar decay. `mech` (2) is a shotgun rack
  or launcher latch pitched down. `impact` (3) is an explosion with a flare crack and fizzle/sizzle layer.
  `burn` is a road flare burning with a sizzle, fading over 2.9 s.
- **Railgun**: `charge` is exactly 1.6 s (the server `chargeSeconds`): a real Tesla-coil buzz under
  tape varispeed rising from 0.55x to 1.25x with two laser charge-ups, ending at its peak. `hold` is a
  1.6 s seamless loop of the same buzz at 1.25x with a railgun charge loop and a synthesized hum. `ready`
  is a clang tick with a synthesized two-tone ping. `fire` (3) layers a U.S. Navy railgun test shot or a
  heavy cannon with a supersonic crack, a high-voltage arc and a close thunder crack (~0.9 s). `tail` (2) is a
  thunder roll. `impact` (2) is heavy metal with an electric discharge.
- **Leadwall**: `fire` (3) is a 12-gauge shot pitched down to ~0.82x with a second shotgun layer and a
  sub thump. `tail` (2) is shotgun decay. `pump` (2) is a pump rack pitched down with a low body copy.
  `ricochet` (4) are four different ricochet recordings. `impact` (3) are short bullet impacts pitched
  down. `reload` is a slow chambering, a shell load and a closing rack.
- **Predator**: `fire` (4) is one heavy round (0.20 s) from an M242 autocannon, a MK 50 33 mm naval gun
  or an M240/DShK, pitched down with a short decay so 8-17 rounds per second stay clear. `spin-up`
  (0.6 s), `spin` (1.18 s loop) and `spin-down` (1.2 s) are an electric motor under varispeed (0.15x to
  0.6x, 0.6x, 0.6x to 0.1x), with a drill layer in the loop and a chain-gun wind-down rattle at the end.
  `casings` (3) are brass casings pitched down to 0.7x. `tail` (2) is heavy machine-gun decay. `reload` is
  a launcher loading sequence with a heavy door slam as the drum lock.
- **Common**: `hit-confirm` (3) are 0.11 s metallic ticks; `doom-confirm` is a deep clang and crunch.

## In-game wiring

`TitanSynth` (`public/js/vehicles/vehicle-audio.js`) plays the groups.
`titan-presentation.js` `init()` loads the bank with
`VehicleAudio.loadTitanBank(TITAN_SAMPLE_MANIFEST)` when the Titanfall client starts, and `dispose()` unloads
it. Failed slots are retried after 5 s and 20 s. Until a group decodes, its previous synthesized voice
plays, so the game never goes silent. Levels are set in `TITAN_WEAPON_MIX` and limits in `TITAN_AUDIO`.

| Cue | Groups | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Shot | `fire` + `tail` (all weapons), `split` | Every primary `shoot` event (`titan-presentation.js` → `titanShot`). The ION split layer plays when the event has more than one path. |
| Mechanics | `t203.mech`, `leadwall.pump` | 0.5 s / 0.28 s after the shot, own titan or within 45 m. |
| Railgun | `charge`, `ready`, `hold` | Mount field 6 of the presented row. The charge take is stretched to the server's 1.6 s and starts at the charge already reached. At full charge the ready cue plays and the hold loop runs until the shot. The report's gain and pitch scale with `event.charge`. |
| Predator | `fire`, `casings`, `tail`, `spinUp`, `spin`, `spinDown` | One round per shot, with at most 5 overlapping. Brass plays every 4th round (own titan or within 40 m). The tail plays 0.16 s after the burst stops (bursts of 3 rounds or more). The spin-up is synced to the server's 0.6 s. The loop holds full spin, and the spin-down follows the barrels down. |
| Reload | `splitter/leadwall/predator.reload` | The own magazine reload starting (mount field 5 rises from 0). |
| Impact | `impact`, `t203.burn` | Hitscan rounds that stopped short of their range, T-203 shells, and Leadwall pellet hits with radius > 0 (`titan-projectile-views.js`). Pellet hits are capped at 3 per 0.12 s. |
| Ricochet | `leadwall.ricochet` | `projectileUpdate` of a tracked pellet, capped at 2 per 0.15 s. |
| Confirm | `hit.confirm`, `hit.doom` | Own effective `vehicle_hit` while piloting a titan (at most one per 0.07 s), and `titan_doomed` by the own rounds. |

- **Own titan:** plays in the head at `selfWeaponGain` 0.8.
- **Other titans:** positioned with HRTF, culled at 280 m (impacts at 90 m, ricochets at 60 m). Past 60 m a low-pass falls from 16 kHz to 2.4 kHz at 280 m, and the tail's share grows with distance. The charge whine and the barrel spin are heard within 55 m.
- **Voice budget:** weapon voices have their own budget of 20, so a Predator burst never starves footfalls or titan cues.
- **Frag fallback removed:** `sfx.explosion` returns early for `t203` and `leadwall`, so a Leadwall shot is no longer ten frag blasts.
